Recipe: Pancakes
=================

Ingredients:
- 1 cup flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp melted butter

Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients.
2. Whisk in milk, egg, and butter.
3. Cook on a hot griddle until bubbles form, then flip.
